# Therma

Therma finds nearby places with the weather you want. Pick where you start, how far you'll drive, a day in the coming week, and warmer or cooler, and Therma shows destinations whose forecast is noticeably better than home's, with drive times and directions. It's free, with no account and no ads.

## Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email **[admin@iterloop.dev](mailto:admin@iterloop.dev)**. It helps to mention your phone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

**Why do I see few or no results?** Therma only shows places whose forecast is noticeably warmer or cooler than where you start. Try a longer drive, another day, or turning on **Include other countries** in Settings.

**Do I have to share my location?** No. Type a city instead. "Use my current location" is optional.

**How do I clear my recent searches?** Settings → **Clear search history**.
