# Therma Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 18, 2026_

Therma helps you find nearby places with the weather you want. We built it to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what the Therma app collects, why, who helps us run it, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- **No account and no sign-up.** The app gives itself a random ID in the background. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
- **Your location is used only when you ask** — to search from where you are, or to show nearby cities first while you type. It is never stored.
- **Your recent searches and settings stay on your phone.**
- **No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.**
- **Crash reports** help us fix bugs. They never include your location, and they aren't connected to the app's ID.

## Who we are

Therma is made by Iterloop, the trading name of Abdullah Sarwari, an independent developer based in California, USA ("we", "us"). For anything about this policy or your data, email **admin@iterloop.dev**.

## What we collect and why

| Data | When | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A random app ID** | Created the first time you open the app | To keep the service working and to limit how many searches one install can run, which protects the service from abuse | Until 12 months after you last use the app, then deleted automatically |
| **Session details** — the IP address and device type of each sign-in session | Each time the app's session is set up or renewed | Security: our hosting provider records them with the session | Deleted together with the app ID |
| **Your searches** — the starting place or position, distance, date, and warmer or cooler | When you search | To find and show results | Used to answer the search and not stored with your ID. Our server logs record the starting city (never your position or your ID) for a limited period, currently up to 7 days, to keep the service running |
| **What you type in the city field** | While you type | To suggest matching cities | Used for that request only; not stored |
| **Your location** (precise or approximate, as you allow) | Only when you tap "Use my current location", or — if you have granted location permission — the phone's last known location while you type a city, so nearby places come first | To search from where you are, and to rank suggestions | Used for that request only; **never stored or logged**. To fetch the weather where you are, we send Google your position rounded to about 1 km |
| **Crash reports** — the error; details of the device and app, such as model, operating system, app version and free memory; the app's last steps before the problem; and a random ID the crash reporter creates for this install | When the app crashes or freezes, or a search or startup fails | To find and fix bugs | Kept by our crash-reporting provider for up to 90 days. They don't include the app ID or your location |

Network requests also reveal your IP address to our hosting and crash-reporting providers, who use it to deliver the service and protect it from abuse.

## What stays on your phone

- Your 50 most recent searches, so you can run one again.
- Your settings: temperature and distance units, and whether to include other countries.
- The sign-in token for the app's random ID.

Your recent searches and settings never leave your phone. The sign-in token is sent only to our hosting provider, with the app's requests. You can clear your search history in Settings, and uninstalling the app removes all of them.

## What we don't do

- We don't show ads, use advertising IDs, or track you across other apps or websites.
- We don't sell or rent personal information, or share it for advertising.
- We don't build profiles of you or your behaviour.
- We don't ask for or keep your name, email address, phone number, contacts or photos.

## Who helps us run Therma

We use a few service providers. They process data only to provide their service to us.

| Provider | What they do for us | What they receive |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** (USA) | Hosting, database and the app's anonymous sign-in | The app ID, session details, search requests, the text you type in the city field, and your location when you use it |
| **Google Maps Platform** | Weather forecasts and driving times | Coordinates of places in our catalog, and — for a "current location" search — your position rounded to about 1 km. Never your app ID. Google's handling is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |
| **Sentry** (USA) | Crash reports | Crash reports, as described above |

Your phone's location and place-name services (from Apple or Google) turn coordinates into place names on your phone. When you tap **Directions**, Apple Maps or Google Maps opens with the destination; when you **Share**, the share sheet passes the image and text to the app you choose. Those apps and services handle your data under their own privacy policies.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users or others.

## Where your data is processed

Our providers process data in the United States. Where data protection laws require it, transfers from other countries rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We process data to provide the service you ask for (your searches and suggestions), on the basis of our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and working (the app ID, session details, server logs and crash reports), and with your consent for location access, which you control in your phone's settings.

## Your choices and rights

- **Location:** you can allow, limit (approximate only) or refuse location access at any time in your phone's settings. Therma works without it — type a city instead.
- **History:** clear it in Settings → Clear search history.
- **Everything on your phone:** uninstall the app.
- **The random app ID:** it is deleted automatically after 12 months without use.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because Therma has no accounts, we usually cannot tell which data belongs to you; if you contact us, we will explain what we hold and help where we can. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

**California residents:** we do not sell or share personal information, and we use location only to provide the search you ask for. The app does not track you across other sites or apps, so it has no different behaviour for "Do Not Track" signals.

## Children

Therma is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our database allows the app to read only what it needs, and access to stored data is limited to our server code.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For a significant change, we will also tell you in the app or on the app's store page before it takes effect.
